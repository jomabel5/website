Message from the Founder & President
Healthcare is not a privilege reserved for a few — it is a human right, and it is the legacy we are called to leave.

JoMabel Healthcare Foundation was born from both personal experience and a calling to serve. As a survivor of cancer and kidney disease, Dr. Clara Ada Ogbaa is committed to ensuring that no one is denied quality healthcare because of geography or circumstance.

Today, that vision is taking shape in Ufuma, Anambra State, Nigeria, where the Foundation is building an integrated healthcare and training ecosystem designed to serve underserved communities for generations.

Dr. Clara Ada Ogbaa, Ed.D.

Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Systems, Not Interventions

JoMabel Healthcare Foundation Inc. is a nonprofit organisation focused on expanding access to high-quality healthcare and training in underserved communities, primarily in Southeastern Nigeria. Its work combines healthcare delivery, workforce development, and infrastructure investment to build lasting systems rather than temporary interventions.

"Residents previously lacked access to a fully equipped medical facility. Preventive and maternal health services were limited. The region faces a significant shortage of trained healthcare professionals."

The Foundation's work is anchored in Ufuma, Anambra State — a community where healthcare gaps have long undermined quality of life and economic potential. JoMabel was created to change that permanently.

Medical Outreach · January 2 Initiative

Partnering with the Nigerian Red Cross to Serve Ufuma

JoMabel team with Nigerian Red Cross Society, Aguata Branch · January 2, 2025

On January 2, JoMabel Healthcare Foundation's team of doctors and nurses partnered with the Nigerian Red Cross Society, Aguata Branch, to host a community medical outreach in Ufuma. The collaboration model had the Red Cross team managing records, registration, and basic screening support, while JoMabel Foundation doctors and nurses handled clinical review and prescriptions.
